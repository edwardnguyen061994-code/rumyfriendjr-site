Delete your child’s data

Last updated {{PUBLISH_DATE}}

You can ask us to delete your child’s RumyFriend Jr account and the information attached to it. You do not need to install or reinstall the app to make this request.

Email {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}} with the subject line “Delete my child’s account”.

Include the child’s player name, and the parent email address used during setup. We may need to confirm you are the parent or guardian on the account before we act, so that another person cannot delete a child’s account.

What gets deleted

The child’s account and the information held against it, including their player name, avatar, friend connections, connection codes and messages they sent.

What may be kept, and why

We may keep a limited record where the law requires it, or where it is needed to handle a safety report, to resolve a dispute, or to keep a blocked contact blocked. Where we keep something, we will tell you what it is and why.

Copies already delivered to another person’s device — for example a message your child sent to a friend — are on that device, and deleting the account here cannot reach into it.

Withdrawing permission instead

If you want to stop a particular feature rather than delete the account, you can withdraw permission for it without deleting anything. Email {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}} and say which feature.
