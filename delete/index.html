Delete your child’s data

Last updated 9 October 2026

You can ask us to review or delete information associated with your child’s RumyFriend Jr play. You do not need to install or reinstall the app to make this request.

The games-only iOS release opens without account setup. It has no Account settings or in-app account-deletion screen. Removing local app or browser data does not delete records already held by a game service.

Email contact@rumyfriendjr.com with the subject line “RumyFriend Jr data request”.

Include the game name, player nickname or room code if available, and an approximate date of play. Do not send a password or a child’s photo. We may ask for more information to locate the relevant records and confirm that the request is authorized.

If your request concerns an account from an earlier test release, say that in your email and include the parent email address used during setup, if there was one.

What a request can cover

Game services can hold guest identifiers, player labels, room information, game contributions and scores. Earlier account-based test releases could also hold account, profile, friend and message information. Contact us to establish which records relate to the request and what can be deleted or corrected.

What may be kept, and why

We may keep a limited record where the law requires it, or where it is needed to handle a safety report, to resolve a dispute, or to keep a blocked contact blocked. Where we keep something, we will tell you what it is and why.

Copies already received by another player or stored on another person’s device cannot necessarily be removed by deleting a game-service record.

Other privacy requests

You can also ask to review or correct information, or ask about stopping collection. Email contact@rumyfriendjr.com and describe the request.
