How a round plays

Simple to start, fun to finish.

1

Start any game

Pick from 32 games and open a room in a tap. Share the link, or let friendly bots fill the seats.

2

Play the same round

Everyone plays together, live — in the room or on their own phones, straight from the browser.

3

Rank up, then repeat

Every game scores you and remembers it. Climb the all-time ranks, meet your people, and run it back.
